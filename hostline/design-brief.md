# Hostline — Design Brief

## Design read
South African SME owners who need a calm, always-on front desk. Emotional register: composed morning switchboard, not a neon AI lab.

## Concept spine
**The morning switchboard** — Hostline is the first light that answers the phone: sea-glass calm, copper wire clarity, no drama.

## Delivery tier
`editorial` lean cinema: typography + atmosphere + micro-motion. No WebGL.

## Locked palette
- Ground mist: `#E8EEF2`
- Paper surface: `#F7FAFC`
- Ink: `#14212B`
- Sea glass accent: `#2A7A6E` (single accent, sat < 80%)
- Mute line: `#8FA3B0`
- Deep panel: `#0F1C24`

Defense: cool mist + sea glass reads like coastal SA morning air over a reception desk, avoiding banned graphite/orange, neon cyan, cream/brass, and purple glow families.

## Locked type
- Display / UI: `Outfit`
- Meta / labels: `IBM Plex Mono`

## Tier-1 technique
**D3 — Kinetic type opener**: Hostline wordmark and headline stagger on load; secondary lines slide on different tracks over a full-bleed mist atmosphere. Enacts the switchboard spine by making the brand voice (typography) the first thing that answers.

## Section plan
1. Hero — full-bleed brand + one line + CTA (asymmetric)
2. Call path — horizontal numbered steps (rail)
3. Grok voice — split text + visual plate
4. Who it's for — asymmetric industry list (not equal cards)
5. Setup — stacked vertical steps
6. Close CTA — single purpose
7. Footer

## CTA inventory
- Primary nav/hero: `Start Hostline` (solid sea-glass block, magnetic hover)
- Secondary hero: `See the desk` (underline draw)
- Close: `Open the desk` (inverted ink block)

## Asset plan
CSS atmosphere + SVG monogram (Higgsfield image kit unavailable in this session). Logo: H mark with soft switchboard arc.
